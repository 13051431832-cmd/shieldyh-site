Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Data Collection & Storage

Deep Desk stores all user data locally on your device in ~/.deepdesk/:

- API keys you configure (DeepSeek, QWEN)
- Conversation history and chat logs
- Application preferences

Third-Party Services

Deep Desk connects to AI API providers (such as DeepSeek or QWEN) at your direction. Your API keys and conversation content are transmitted directly to these services according to their respective privacy policies. Deep Desk does not route data through any intermediate server.

Analytics & Tracking

Deep Desk does not collect telemetry, analytics, or usage data. No crash reports, no analytics SDKs, no tracking of any kind.

In-App Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Deep Desk does not have access to your payment information.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data remains on your device. Uninstalling the app and removing the ~/.deepdesk/ directory permanently deletes all data.
